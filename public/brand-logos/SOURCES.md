# Brand asset sources

These marks identify tools used in Golddigi projects. Their trademarks belong to their respective owners. Display does not imply partnership or certification.

- Next.js: Vercel / Next.js brand asset.
- Sanity: official `sanity-io/logos` repository, `src/sanityMonogram.tsx` and `src/colors.ts`.
- GitHub: GitHub mark from GitHub's brand resources.
- n8n, OpenAI, WhatsApp, Supabase, and PostgreSQL: their official integration assets as distributed in n8n's public integrations repository.
- Gmail, Google Drive, and Google Workspace: official Google Workspace product and wordmark assets.

The SVG files are served locally by this website. They are presentation assets and are not executable code.
